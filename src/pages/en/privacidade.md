## What is collected

This site collects no personal data. There are no forms and no tracking cookies.

## Contact

Contact happens by email: the link opens your own provider (such as Gmail) with the recipient filled in. The message is sent from your account and is used only to reply to you.

## Analytics

Only aggregated, anonymous visit metrics are collected, without cookies and without identifying you.

## Your rights

You can ask for any message you sent me to be deleted by replying to that email.
