## Context

Small businesses had websites flagged as dangerous by Google Safe Browsing, which kills traffic and scares customers away.

## Problem

- Malicious code injected into files and the database.
- Outdated plugins and dependencies reopened the hole after cleanup.
- Urgency: every flagged day means lost sales.

## Solution

A rapid-response process: isolate, find the entry point, clean up, harden the environment and request a Google review.

## Technical decisions

1. **Investigate before cleaning.** Comparison against known-good versions and log analysis to find the root cause.
2. **Hardening.** Updates, removal of unused components, least-privilege file permissions and rotation of every credential.
3. **Repeatable checklist** to meet the 1-business-day SLA with consistent quality.

## Outcome

Websites back to safe browsing status on time, with no reinfection through the same hole.
